## 1. Narrative invariant

WIND is situation-first visual development. Preserve the hierarchy:

`hero event → evidence → consequence → forward pressure`

Signal Stage should reveal that hierarchy over time. It must not flatten a WIND spread into a gallery or split every evidence panel into equal-weight slides.

## 2. Source expectations

A finalized WIND package should identify:

- spread title and narrative function;
- approved full spread master;
- clean master when runtime text will be dynamic;
- approved baked-text policy when editorial typography is part of the spread;
- hero region and evidence regions;
- canonical/proposed/reserved status of visible details;
- alt text and any approved transcript;
- approval evidence and checksums.

When these are absent, build a blocked ingestion spec and list them. Do not manufacture approval.

## 3. Mode mapping

- **Mosaic** — default for a hero image supported by evidence panels. Keep the hero dominant; activate evidence in causal order.
- **Focus** — use when one cinematic image carries the spread and detail is discovered through a restrained camera path.
- **Sequence** — use when the spread already encodes a clear event progression or forensic frame series.
- **Stage** — use when one stable visual supports timed testimony, dialogue, or analytic emphasis.
- **Drift** — use for atmospheric ordinary-life or aftermath spreads with subtle environmental change. Do not use drift to fake character animation.
- **Break** — use for maps, dossiers, documents, feeds, public artifacts, or a deliberate interface rupture.

Choose the mode from the spread's narrative job, not its aspect ratio.

## 4. Direction doctrine

- Start with observation; delay explanation.
- Let the hero image own 50–70% of perceptual emphasis.
- Activate evidence panels only when they change understanding.
- Treat maps, timestamps, source labels, and confidence levels as evidence with reading order.
- Use motion to reveal a relationship, not to make a static spread feel busy.
- Use one dominant motion class per beat; two only when causally linked.
- Keep mobile crops honest. Guide across the approved spread rather than regenerating it vertically.
- Preserve ambiguity marked as proposed or reserved.

## 5. Text policy

WIND spreads often contain editorial labels, maps, comments, timelines, or annotations. Classify each visible text family:

- **diegetic** — exists inside the event, such as a phone comment or sign;
- **editorial evidence** — title, timestamp, coordinates, confidence label, measurement;
- **performance copy** — dialogue, caption, interpretation, or narration controlled by Signal Stage.

Use `hybrid` when diegetic/editorial evidence is approved in the image but performance copy remains modular. Use `baked-editorial` only when the complete typographic composition is itself the approved art object.

Do not duplicate baked text as a cue. Do not transcribe illegible pseudo-text into canon.

## 6. Ingestion rejection triggers

Reject or block a source when it shows:

- a panel layout different from the approved composition;
- missing, malformed, or disconnected focal anatomy;
- identity drift or unapproved wardrobe change;
- duplicated, floating, mirrored, or missing tracked props;
- changed room geography, device orientation, or handedness;
- generated dialogue, labels, watermarks, or readable pseudo-text in a clean master;
- an approved symbol altered during cleanup;
- a QA proof or rejected candidate presented as the final source;
- checksum mismatch or absent local bytes;
- an attempted generative unlettering of a finalized spread.

These are upstream production failures. Signal Stage may not repair them during ingestion.
