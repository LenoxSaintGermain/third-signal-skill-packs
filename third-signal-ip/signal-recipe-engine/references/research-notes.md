# Signal Recipe Engine — research notes

These notes record the principles translated into the local recipe system. They are a research trail, not a style-imitation list.

## Sequential art and graphic novels

- Scott McCloud's *Understanding Comics* frames comics as a medium whose meaning is built through visual sequence, closure, time, space, word/image relationships, and reader participation. The engine translates that into **Sequential Reveal**: panels, gutters, eye path, silence, and page turns are active narrative machinery.
- Graphic-novel scripting practice emphasizes that the page is a fixed visual container and that page turns can function as suspense or reveal devices. The engine therefore requires page-level intent before panel-level prose and asks what information changes at the turn.
- Creator Greg Pak describes using page endings for mini-cliffhangers: a question, unfinished thought, or action completed after the turn. This becomes a useful tactic, not a mandatory formula.

## Action and combat

- Final Draft's fight-scene guide identifies clarity, escalation, character expression, visual writing, and inevitability as simultaneous requirements. It describes the underlying unit as cause → consequence → shift rather than a stunt list.
- Writing Excuses' fight-scene discussion warns against inserting action that does not tell the story and against writing a blow-by-blow sequence without a narrative reason. The engine translates this into the **Pressure Combat** test: every major beat must change the strategic or emotional state.
- For fantasy action, heightened spectacle increases the need for clear geography and readable turns. The engine therefore asks for an arena, a focus character, a visible limitation, and one iconic turn.

## Magic and action-fantasy systems

- Brandon Sanderson's official discussion of magic systems argues that conflict-solving power should track reader understanding, that limitations are more generative than powers, and that existing systems should be expanded and interconnected before new systems are added.
- The engine generalizes those ideas into **Constraint-Bearing System**, while deliberately adding access, visible signature, misuse, cultural meaning, and failure so the system is not reduced to a power spreadsheet.
- These are guidelines, not universal laws. A recipe may break a rule deliberately if it records the intended effect and the risk.

## Local synthesis

The distinctive local move is to join sequential-art mechanics, action logic, system constraints, and publishing provenance into one selector. A piece is not considered “unique” because it has unusual nouns or a fashionable visual skin. It is unique when a controlled mutation changes how the audience receives evidence, how the system creates pressure, or how the artifact records consequence.
